An Essay With No Media

By the quiet desk

This page exists to test what happens when there is nothing to embed. No iframes, no video tags, no images, no audio. Just words, and the space around them.

The absence of media is itself a design choice. A reader here cannot be distracted by a player, cannot be sold a recommendation, cannot be pulled sideways into a thumbnail grid. The essay is simply a long room with a single sentence as its floor plan.

If your extractor believes every article should contain media, this is the page that should teach it otherwise.
