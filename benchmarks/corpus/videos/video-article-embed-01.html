How to Fold a Paper Airplane That Flies Home

From the Sunday craft desk

Start with a sheet of A4, not printer paper with a crease down the middle. Fold the top corners to the centerline, then fold the whole triangle down. Fold the new top corners to the center again, but leave the little triangle exposed; it becomes the lock that holds everything.

Watch the demonstration before you fold:

The plane returns home only if the wingtips are reflexed upward by about two degrees. Test in a corridor without drafts first.
