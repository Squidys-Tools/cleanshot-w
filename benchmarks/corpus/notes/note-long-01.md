# A Longer Journal Entry

July 30, 2026

Today I spent the afternoon in the reading room of the central library,
the one with the clock that doesn't work. I read maybe twenty pages and
watched the light move across the floor in the way light does when you
give it time.

I have been thinking about what it means to keep a library of my own
things. Not the books I own, but the pieces of the internet I have
decided are worth remembering: an essay about attention, a photograph of
a room I will never visit, a recipe that made Tuesday better.

There is a difference between saving and filing. Filing is deciding where
something belongs. Saving is deciding it belongs somewhere. The first
asks you to be an archivist; the second only asks you to be a person who
notices.

I want to notice more. I want the collection to grow the way a garden
grows, which is to say messily, with surprises, and with a few things
that never quite made sense to plant but turned out to be the best part.

Tomorrow: water the plants, send the postcard, and read one more page
than yesterday.
