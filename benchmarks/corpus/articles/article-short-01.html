The Case for Slower News

By Mira Alderton · July 14, 2026

The news cycle moves at the speed of a tap, but understanding moves at the speed of a walk. This short essay argues that most of us are over-informed and under-considered, and that a deliberately slower reading habit is the cheaper remedy.

Three habits help: read one long piece a week, write one sentence about it in your own words, and let a headline age a day before believing it. None of these require a new app or a subscription.

Speed makes us feel caught up. Slowness makes us actually understand. If you only take one thing from this post, take that.
