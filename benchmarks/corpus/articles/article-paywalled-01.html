The Slow Reading Manifesto

By Ingrid Solberg

The full text of this essay is available to subscribers. What follows is the first paragraph, offered as a taste.

Reading is the only common activity that gets worse when it gets faster. A novel read at double speed is not a novel; it is a summary wearing a costume. And yet the entire apparatus of modern media is built to make us read faster, skim harder, and finish sooner, as though finishing were the point.
