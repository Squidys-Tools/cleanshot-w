One-Pan Miso Butter Salmon

Recipe by June Park · Serves 2 · 25 minutes

This is the weeknight recipe that earns its pan back. A single skillet does the whole job, and the sauce works on almost anything else you happen to own: chicken, tofu, or just a bowl of rice with extra butter.

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (about 150 g each), skin on
  • 2 leeks, white and light green parts, halved lengthwise
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 200 g cooked jasmine rice, for serving
  • Salt, pepper, and sesame seeds to finish

Method

  1. Mash the miso, butter, honey, and rice vinegar into a paste. Season the salmon with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with a little oil. Add the leeks cut-side down and cook until charred, about 4 minutes, then set aside.
  3. Sear the salmon skin-side down for 3 minutes, flip, and smear the miso butter over the flesh. Lower the heat, cover, and cook 4 to 6 minutes until just cooked through.
  4. Serve over rice with the leeks and a spoon of the pan sauce. Finish with sesame seeds.

Notes

White miso is milder; brown miso makes the sauce saltier, so taste before adding more salt. If you double the recipe, brown the salmon in batches so the pan stays hot.
