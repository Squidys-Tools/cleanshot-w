Quick access card (CleanShot's signature). Right after capture, a small floating card lands bottom-right: thumbnail on top, Copy and Save as the two big actions, Annotate and Pin as icons, capture meta beneath. It is the only surface that appears without being asked for — so it stays small, quiet, and dismissible (Esc or auto-hide).
